The Miswak Stick: reversing an organic decline

E-commerceAug – Oct 2026~9 weeks
  1. ProblemOrganic traffic on this miswak store had been sliding for months, and nobody knew why.
  2. DiagnosisConfirmed no manual actions in Search Console. Audited the backlink profile and found a large volume of auto-generated spam links. Found that unpublished /de and /it market pages had left the URL structure broken.
  3. ActionsRestructured the market URLs, realigned page content to target keywords by intent, and rebuilt internal linking so authority flows to product and category pages.
  4. ResultOrganic traffic nearly doubled by end of October, and the site is now cited by ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode and Gemini.
